## Introduction

As the marketing team of **SmartBuddy**, an innovative home assistant designed to redefine how households interact with technology, you are tasked with ensuring a successful product launch. SmartBuddy integrates voice control, personalized recommendations, and home automation into one sleek device, aiming to capture the attention of a tech-savvy audience.

To achieve this, your team is leveraging cutting-edge AI tools to enhance every stage of the campaign. From creating dynamic promotional content to automating workflows and improving team collaboration, AI becomes a crucial ally in delivering efficiency and creativity. By integrating tools like Synthesia for video production, tl;dv for meeting transcription, and Make for automation, the team can focus on strategy while minimizing repetitive tasks.

This initiative not only demonstrates the power of AI in marketing and operations but also positions **SmartBuddy** as a symbol of innovation—both in its features and in how it is brought to market.

## **Iteration 1: Content Creation with AI Tools**

### **Context**
You are part of the marketing team of a startup launching a new gadget called **SmartBuddy**, an intelligent home assistant that combines voice control, automation, and entertainment. Your task is to create promotional materials using AI tools.

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dbjlaop5w/image/upload/v1733302519/codeskills/generative-ai/Foundations%20of%20NLP%20and%20Large%20Language%20Models%20%28LLMs%29/wfeeolpsxhxm6jdd513h.png)

### **Activities**

1. **Promotional Video Creation with Synthesia**
   - Create a short video (30-60 seconds) featuring an avatar explaining SmartBuddy’s key features:
     - Voice control.
     - Home automation.
     - Personalized recommendations.
   - Customize the avatar, language, and style to target a young, tech-savvy audience.
   - Export the video and capture a screenshot of the result.

2. **Editing an Interview about SmartBuddy with Descript**
   - Use a fictional audio or video file where a company representative talks about SmartBuddy (you can record it yourself or use a provided clip).
   - Edit the file to remove long pauses and errors using the transcription feature.
   - Add a new audio segment with Overdub to emphasize a key message, like "SmartBuddy: Your intelligent ally."

3. **Explainer Video Creation with Lumen5**
   - Turn a promotional text (e.g., "5 reasons to choose SmartBuddy") into a dynamic video:
     - Include relevant animations and customize the branding (colors, fonts).
   - Ensure to add a call to action at the end of the video, such as "Learn more on our website."

## **Iteration 3: Automating Processes with Make**

### **Context**
As part of the SmartBuddy launch campaign, you want to automate repetitive tasks to save time and ensure consistency. Use **Make** to connect tools and streamline the process.

![](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/31ee7a4e-a57d-4d54-8351-9cbde7505a74)

### **Activities**

1. **Task 1: Saving Client Documents in Google Drive**
     Your company needs to organize all client documents in Google Drive. The goal is to save every incoming document into a designated folder, creating subfolders for each client automatically.

   - **Exercise**:  
     - Create an automation in **Make** to save all incoming client documents into Google Drive.  
     - Test the automation to ensure subfolders are created properly and files are saved in the correct location.


2. **Task 2: Customizing Document Storage for Specific Clients**  
     After implementing the basic document storage automation, the process needs to be refined to save files only for specific clients, such as those labeled as "VIP" or with a specific email domain.

   - **Exercise**:  
     - Customize the existing automation to filter files for selected clients only.  
     - Test the scenario to ensure:  
       - Files for non-selected clients are ignored.  
       - Only files for the specific clients are saved in their respective subfolders.


3. **Task: Automating Meeting Summaries and Notifications**
     After each marketing meeting recorded on Google Meet, the following steps need to happen automatically:
     - Transcription and summarization with tl;dv.
     - Summaries are emailed to all team members.
     - A Slack notification is sent to highlight action items.

   - **Exercise**:
     - Design and implement the automation scenario in **Make**.
     - Test the scenario with a sample meeting to ensure:
       - The summary is correctly generated and sent via email.
       - Action items are accurately posted in Slack.

### **Expected Deliverables**
- **Scenario Files**: Screenshots or export files of the scenarios created in Make. Put them in `student_deliverables` folder.
